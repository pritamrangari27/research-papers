# An ESP32-Based Voice Assistant Integrating Gemini AI

This folder contains the published research paper titled:

**An ESP32-Based Voice Assistant Integrating Gemini AI and Mobile Speech Recognition**

---

## 📝 Publication Details
- **Journal:** International Journal of Advanced Research in Science, Communication and Technology (IJARSCT)
- **Volume / Issue:** Vol. 5, Issue 8
- **Year:** 2025
- **Authors:** Prof. Vikas Desai, Amol Waghmare, Sandesh Shinde, Pritam Rangari, Ganesh Shejul, Dishant Thakur, Suraj Valunj

---

## 📌 Abstract
This research introduces a **low-cost, customizable voice assistant** using the ESP32 microcontroller, integrating **Gemini AI** for natural language understanding and a mobile app for **speech recognition**. The system demonstrates adaptability and scalability for embedded IoT applications.

📎 Official PDF: https://www.ijarsct.co.in/Paper25577.pdf
