## 📝 Publication Details
- **Journal:** International Journal of Advanced Research in Science, Communication and Technology (IJARSCT)
- **Volume / Issue:** Vol. 5, Issue 2
- **Year:** 2025
- **Authors:** Prof. Pritesh Patil, Pritam Rangari, Sharvari Shalgar, Nirja Sartale

---

## 📌 Abstract
This research presents a **web-based crime management system** designed for Pune City to improve the efficiency of crime reporting and record management. The system allows citizens to file complaints online with supporting evidence, while police officials can manage FIRs, criminal records, and investigation details through a centralized database-driven platform. The solution enhances transparency, accessibility, and operational efficiency in crime administration.
